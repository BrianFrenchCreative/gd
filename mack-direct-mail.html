I. About the Project

  • 🎯 The Challenge

    Mack Trucks needed to gather actionable intelligence on fleet sizes and future purchasing plans. Targeted to 4,000 selected historical, current, and competitive Mack owners, the challenge was designing a compelling incentive-driven package that bridged the physical and digital divide, seamlessly guiding users from a physical mailer to an online fulfillment process.

  • 👤 My Role

    Lead Designer

    Print Layout, UI/UX Design, Direct Mail Strategy, Campaign Mapping

  • ⚙️ The Process
    • Print Production: Designed the physical package containing a personalized letter, physical survey, and a $20 MackShop.com gift card incentive, ensuring high legibility and clear calls-to-action.
    • Digital Execution: Mapped and designed the digital counterpart, including the initial survey email drop, a seamless 4-step digital fulfillment process, and the final automated reward email.

  • 📈 The Outcome

    The campaign was a massive success, experiencing a high 25% return rate. Over 1,000 respondents completed the physical mail survey or its identical online version. These high-value results were fed directly to local Mack dealers, equipping them to follow up with customers based on exact fleet needs and purchasing timelines.

II. The Direct Mail Package

Incentivizing the Data Capture

To drive a massive 25% return rate, the physical collateral relied on clear, distraction-free typography and a prominent upfront incentive. The layout guides the reader seamlessly from the personalized letter straight into the survey mechanics, minimizing friction and encouraging an immediate return via the provided Business Reply Mail.
